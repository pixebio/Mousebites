## 🎮 Controls & Keybinds 🎮

We keep things super simple so you can focus on being cute and getting huge!

*   ⬆️ **Arrow Keys** OR ⌨️ **WASD** - Move your little snake friend around!
*   ⏸️ **P** - Pause the game (perfect for when you need a quick snack break!)
*   🔄 **R** - Restart the game (because we all make oopsies sometimes!)

---

## 🌱 How to Play 🌱

1.  **Start the Game:** Launch the game and pick your difficulty. 
2.  **Munch the Yummies:** Guide your snake to eat the little snacks on the grid. Every time you eat, you grow! 🍎
3.  **Don't Bonk!:** Watch out for the walls, and *definitely* don't bite your own tail! If you crash, the "You Died" screen will pop up to show your final score. 🥺
4.  **Try Again!:** Hit that adorable little *Restart* button (or let Auto Replay do its magic) and beat your last score!

Want to run this cozy little game on your own machine? It's super easy!

2. **Open the Folder:**
```bash

```


3. **Launch with Live Server:**
Pop open your favorite code editor (like VS Code), right-click `index.html`, and select **"Open with Live Server"**. (You'll see it running smoothly at `http://127.0.0.1:5500/index.html` just like in our screenshots! ✨)
